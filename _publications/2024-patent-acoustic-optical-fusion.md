Patent **CN202311353714**, developed at Shanghai Ocean University (advisor:
Prof. Bowen Xing). Covers an acoustic-optical fusion perception framework that
combines optical images with P360 sonar data (2° horizontal beamwidth) for accurate
target recognition and size measurement in low-light/turbid waters.
